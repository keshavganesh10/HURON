# Huron Residences

**The Art of Frictionless Living.**

Huron Residences is an ultra-premium showcase and private reservation platform redefining modern luxury real estate. Engineered with an elite private banking aesthetic, it presents a vertically integrated ecosystem where bespoke architecture, tailored capital, and lifelong engineering converge to protect wealth and restore time.

> **100% Bespoke & Frictionless.** Eliminating the middleman through unified development, lending, and aftercare. Explore available plots and secure private reservations through a discrete, stealth-wealth digital experience.

`TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Lovable` `Private Access`

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏛 Ecosystem Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Residences Showcase** | High-fidelity interactive plots featuring modern architectural pavilions, spatial specifications, and curated interior finishes. |
| **Huron Capital** | Bespoke private lending solutions and tailored financing frameworks integrated directly into the acquisition workflow. |
| **The Aftercare App** | Lifelong engineering management, proactive maintenance protocols, and white-glove concierge services. |
| **Private Access Engine** | Discrete, high-touch pre-booking and reservation workflows engineered exclusively for verified clients. |

### 💎 Architectural & Design Standards

Refined design modifiers that deliver an executive digital standard:

* **Stealth-Wealth Aesthetic** — Built with deep slate foundations (`#111215`), warm off-white typography (`#F5F5F7`), and subtle metallic bronze accents.
* **Blurred-Glass Navigation** — Sticky backdrop-blur navigation bar with fluid drawer transitions for mobile responsiveness.
* **Cinematic Hero Display** — Shallow-focus architectural visualisations complemented by micro-interactions.
* **Zero-Placeholder Precision** — Populated with definitive, high-fidelity luxury copy and structured plot datasets without generic placeholder text.
